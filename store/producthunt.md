## Description (260 characters max)

```
A free, open source Chrome extension that blocks distracting sites in two clicks. Open one during a session and you see the time left and why you're doing this, not the feed. Ending early means typing a sentence by hand. No account, no tracking.
```

## Maker's first comment

Adapt it to your own words before posting.

```
Hey Product Hunt 👋

I'm Maxime. I built WeDeepFocus because "just one more scroll" kept eating my mornings, and the blockers I tried were either too easy to switch off or too annoying to keep.

So I kept it simple:
• Two clicks to start a session (15 min, 30 min, 1 h)
• Open a blocked site and you see the time left and your own "why", one sentence you picked or wrote
• Ending early means one honest question, then typing a sentence by hand. No copy-paste 😅

It's free, open source (MIT) and collects nothing: no account, no analytics, your settings stay in your browser.

I'd love your feedback: what would make you keep it on every day?
```
